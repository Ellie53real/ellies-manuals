From *Sonic R*'s [Sega Saturn boxart](https://info.sonicretro.org/images/9/9a/Sonic_R_Saturn_US_Cover_Back.jpg): The world's most notorious speed demon pushes the 32-bit speed limit into extreme overdrive in a twisting, turning, upside-down-corkscrewing race for the finish line. Compete with 4 different characters - PLUS special "hidden" track smokers - and satisfy your need for speed through 5 blistering circuits. This wicked band of Mach-maniacs is sporting enough torque to keep you flossing bug guts out of your teeth for days.

*Sonic R* is a racing game starring Sonic and friends, with 5 tracks and 10 playable characters.

In Archipelago, the goal of this game is to collect up to 7 of the Chaos Emeralds (the exact number can be controlled with a YAML setting), and get 1st place on Radiant Emerald.

Every character in the game is an item in the pool - you start with Amy Rose, and faster characters logically unlock the ability to win on later stages.

IMPORTANT: Due to the way the game handles Sonic Tokens (all Sonic Tokens on a course disappear once the character relevant to the course is unlocked), you are expected to start from a fresh save file.
