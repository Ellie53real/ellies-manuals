# *Peggle Extreme*
From *Peggle Extreme*'s [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3483/Peggle_Extreme/): Mayday! The Peggle Institute has been invaded by a host of other-worldly creatures. It's up to you, Peggle Master, to report to duty and shoot and bounce the critters to oblivion. Take your best shot with energizing arcade fun, now exclusively themed with art and sounds from *The Orange Box*!

*Peggle Extreme* is a spin-off of *Peggle*, with a set of 10 levels themed around *Half-Life 2: Episode 2*, *Portal*, and *Team Fortress 2*, made to promote the (at the time) new Valve collection, *The Orange Box*.

In Archipelago, the goal of this game is to complete level 2-5: "I Love Crates".

Locations consist of various things achieved within each level of the game:
* Achieving combos of pegs hit in a single shot
* Achieving different thresholds on the Fever Meter
* Hitting Style Shots worth 25,000 or more points
* Clearing the level (hitting every orange peg)
* * Optionally, Full Clears (which entail hitting _every_ peg in a level) can also be enabled!

This game's Manual is written to have logic that functions similarly to that of existing (non-Manual) APWorlds for *Peggle Deluxe* and *Peggle Nights*.
